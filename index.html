Privacy Policy: PDF Signer

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Summary: PDF Signer works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics.

Who we are

PDF Signer (Android package com.kunaltayade.pdfsigner) is developed by Kunal Tayade (“we”, “us”). Contact: kunaltayade@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store on our servers, or have access to any information about you or your documents. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

Data that stays on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read this storage, and we never receive it:

  • PDFs and images you open, and the versions you save
  • Signatures you choose to save for reuse
  • A list of your documents and their edit history

This data stays until you remove it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

When you choose Save to phone, a copy is written to your device's Documents/PDF Signer folder, where other apps on the device and your file manager can see it. When you choose Share, the file goes to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or Gmail), and that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup, encrypted and tied to your Google account. This is managed by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot access it. You can turn this off in your phone's settings under Google → Backup.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: light haptic feedback when you place items.
  • Storage (Android 10 and older only): to save copies to the Documents folder when you ask the app to.

Files are opened only through the Android system file picker, or when you use “Open with” or “Share” from another app. The app can access only the files you pick.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Security

Your documents are stored in app-private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to protect.

Your choices

You can delete any document or saved signature inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
